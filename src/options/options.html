            About Moat

            Moat blocks ads, trackers, and hijacked popups on Chrome and Firefox. The full README explains how the blocking, popup firewall, and cosmetic filtering work.

            Keyboard shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+M (Cmd+Shift+M on Mac) toggles protection on and off. Rebind it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

            Privacy

            Everything on this page is stored locally in your browser and never sent to Moat's developer. The privacy policy covers the few opt-in features -- breach checking and settings sync -- that contact anyone else.

            Filter updates: once a day Moat fetches its redirect list and any emergency fixes from GitHub. That request carries no browsing data.

            Breach checking: with this on, only the first five characters of a password's SHA-1 hash go to Have I Been Pwned -- never the full hash, the password, or anything to Moat.

            Settings sync: with this on, your settings travel through your browser's own sync account (Google's or Mozilla's), not a server Moat runs.

            Managed install: your organization set the policies shown here. Your administrator can see and change those policies, but not your browsing history.

            Debugging

            The rule-match logger is a development tool that shows which compiled rules matched which network requests. It only appears when Moat is loaded unpacked.

            Backup & restore

            Save your settings to a file, or load one back in. The fingerprint-resistance identifier is never included.

            Sync settings across devices

            Mirrors your settings through your browser's own sync account. A new install picks them up once; it doesn't merge edits made on two devices at the same time.
