            Your organization manages this copy of Moat and set the policies shown here. Your administrator can see and change those policies, but not your browsing history.

            What Moat connects to

            • Blocking & filteringNothing. Runs entirely on your device. Nobody always on
            • StatisticsNothing leaves your device. Counts stay stored locally. Nobody always on
            • Daily list updatesFilter fixes and today's phishing and scam lists, downloaded once a day as signed files. Nothing about you is sent. GitHub Pages, a file host on
            • Catch hidden trackersWeb addresses that might be a tracker in disguise, just before they load. Chrome only. Cloudflare off
            • Warn about leaked passwordsA short scrambled fragment of a password, never the password itself Have I Been Pwned off
            • Settings syncYour Moat settings. No browsing data. Your browser vendor off
            • Problem reportsOnly when you press Send: the site, what went wrong, your note, and Moat's version and settings. You see all of it first. Moat's developer, privately when you send
            • Organization security eventsFor threats Moat blocked: a category, risk level, time and domain. Never the full address or the page. Your organization's own server set by your IT team
            Appearance Light or dark. System follows your device.
