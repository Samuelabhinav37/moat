          About Moat

          What this version is, and everything that ever leaves your device.

          Feature What's sent Who receives it Default
          Blocking & filtering Nothing. Runs entirely on your device. Nobody always on
          Filter fixes One small signed file, downloaded once a day. It carries nothing about you. GitHub Pages (file hosting) on
          Breach checking A short scrambled fragment of a password, never the password itself Have I Been Pwned off
          Settings sync Your Moat settings. No browsing data. Your browser vendor off
          Statistics Nothing leaves your device. Counts stay stored locally. Nobody always on

          Welcome to Moat

          Blocks ads, trackers, and hijacked popups.

          Already protecting you
          Ads, trackers and known-malicious sites blocked Balanced filter lists, on by default
          YouTube video ads dimmed automatically In-stream ads are grayed out while they play
          Popup and redirect firewall Always on. Hijacked tabs are closed automatically.
          Worth turning on
          Block device fingerprinting Off by default since it can occasionally affect a CAPTCHA
          Warn about leaked passwords Off by default. Only a scrambled fragment of a password ever leaves your device.

          Both are one click away in Settings.

          Ctrl+Shift+M toggles protection on and off, anytime.
