        About Moat

        Moat blocks ads, trackers, and hijacked popups on Chrome and Firefox. The full README explains how the blocking, popup firewall, and cosmetic filtering work.

        Keyboard shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+M (Cmd+Shift+M on Mac) toggles protection on and off. Rebind it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

        Privacy

        Everything here stays on your device -- none of it is sent to Moat's developer. Only two optional features (breach checking and settings sync) ever talk to anyone else; see the privacy policy for details.

        Filter updates: once a day, Moat checks for a fresher block list and any urgent fixes. That check doesn't send any of your browsing history.

        Breach checking: with this on, only a short, scrambled piece of your password goes to Have I Been Pwned -- never the real password, and nothing goes to Moat at all.

        Settings sync: with this on, your settings travel through your browser's own sync account (Google's or Mozilla's), not a server Moat runs.

        Managed install: your organization set the policies shown here. Your administrator can see and change those policies, but not your browsing history.

        Debugging

        The rule-match logger is a development tool that shows which compiled rules matched which network requests. It only appears when Moat is loaded unpacked.

        Backup & restore

        Save your settings to a file, or load one back in later. Your fingerprint-protection ID is never included, so it won't carry over.

        Sync settings across devices

        Copies your settings using your browser's own sync account, like your Google or Firefox account. A new install picks them up once, but editing settings on two devices at the same time won't merge -- one will overwrite the other.
