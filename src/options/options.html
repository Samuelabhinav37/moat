Filter Lists

The rule sets Moat compiles into the browser's own blocker. Every one ships inside the extension itself — nothing is fetched by your browser to make them work.

Some of Moat's filter lists couldn't turn on. Browsers limit how many blocking rules extensions can use in total, and that limit is easy to hit if you have another ad blocker or privacy extension running too. Try turning one of them off, or switch off a list below.

        Sync settings across devices

        Hands your Moat settings to your browser account to carry. No browsing data — but it is the one thing on this page that sends anything anywhere.

        Recipient is your browser vendor on this build. Your settings become subject to their retention, not Moat's — Moat has no server and cannot delete them for you.

        What a backup contains
        —protection settings
        —custom rules
        —site exceptions
        —filter list choices
        —no browsing history, ever
        —no block counts or statistics

        Blocking & filtering Nothing — runs entirely on your device Nobody always on
