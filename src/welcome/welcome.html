    How Moat works

    Moat stops ads and trackers before they download, not after they appear.

    1. The page asks for thingsImages, scripts, ads and trackers, often hundreds per page.
    2. Moat checks each one on your deviceAgainst about 310,000 rules from public filter lists like AdGuard's. Nothing is sent anywhere to decide.
    3. Matches never loadAnd neither does anything they would have loaded next.
    4. Leftovers get tidiedEmpty ad boxes fold away. Cookie banners get a “reject” where the site allows it.
